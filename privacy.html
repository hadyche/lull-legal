Lull

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Lull ("the app," "we," "us") is an iPhone app that lets you record sleep and wellness affirmations in your own voice and play them back over calming ambient sounds. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. Your privacy is central to how Lull is built.

The short version:

Everything you create in Lull — your voice recordings, settings, and listening stats — stays on your device. We do not collect it, upload it, or send it to any server, and no one else can hear your voice.

Information stored on your device

  • Voice recordings. Affirmations you record are saved as audio files in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • Settings & preferences. Your name, chosen theme, ambient sound, sleep timer, wake-alarm settings, and reminder preferences.
  • Listening stats. A local record of how long affirmations played, used to show your streak and weekly activity.

All of the above is stored only on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Microphone

Lull requests microphone access solely so you can record affirmations. Recording only happens when you tap the record button. The resulting audio is stored on your device and is not shared.

Notifications

If you enable the nightly reminder, streak reminder, or gentle wake alarm, Lull schedules local notifications on your device. These are handled entirely by iOS; no notification data is sent to us.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Lull Premium, the purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data we do not collect

Lull does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not build a profile about you.

Deleting your data

You can remove everything at any time from You → Account & privacy → Delete all data, which erases your recordings and stats from the device. Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes all of its data.

Children

Lull is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Lull adds optional cloud backup or account sync in the future, this policy will be updated to describe that clearly before the feature is enabled, and any such syncing will be opt-in.
