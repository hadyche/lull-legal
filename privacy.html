Lunaffirm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Lunaffirm ("the app," "we," "us") is an iPhone app that lets you record sleep and wellness affirmations in your own voice and play them back over calming ambient sounds. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. Your privacy — and especially the privacy of your voice — is central to how Lunaffirm is built.

The short version:

Without an account, everything stays on your device. If you create an account, your recordings, settings, and stats are backed up to our secure cloud so you can restore them on a new phone — encrypted in transit and at rest, and readable only by you. We do not sell your personal information, and we never share your recordings with advertisers.

Using Lunaffirm without an account

If you don't sign in, your voice recordings, settings, and listening stats are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else, and no one else can hear your voice.

When you create an account

If you sign in (with Apple, Google, or email), we back up the following to your account so it can follow you to a new device:

  • Voice recordings — the affirmation audio files you record.
  • Settings & preferences — your name, theme, sounds, timers, and reminders.
  • Listening stats — your streak and per-day play activity.
  • Account identifiers — the email address or provider ID used to sign in.

This data is stored on our behalf by Supabase (our cloud hosting and storage provider). It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, and access is restricted to your own account through per-user security rules — other users cannot see your data, and we do not listen to your recordings.

How we use your information

We use it solely to provide the app: to store and restore your recordings, sync your settings and stats across your devices, sign you in, and provide support. We do not use your recordings or personal data for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. The only sharing that happens is with the service providers below, who process data strictly on our behalf to run the app.

Service providers

  • Supabase — cloud database and storage for your account, recordings, and stats.
  • Resend — sends account emails (confirmation, password reset).
  • Apple & Google — process "Sign in with Apple" and "Continue with Google" if you use them.
  • Apple / RevenueCat — process subscription purchases; we do not receive your payment card details.

These providers are bound by their own privacy and security commitments and may only use the data to provide their service to us.

Business transfers

If Lunaffirm or Chehab Group is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. If that happens, we will notify you, and any acquirer will be required to honor this Privacy Policy or a policy at least as protective of your information.

Data retention & deletion

Cloud data is kept while your account is active. You can remove everything at any time from You → Account & privacy → Delete all data, which deletes your recordings and stats from both your device and our cloud. Signing out leaves your backup in place; deleting all data removes it. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy.

Your choices

  • Export your data as JSON from You → Account & privacy → Export data.
  • Delete all your data (device + cloud) from the same screen.
  • Use the app account-free to keep everything on-device only.

Microphone

Lunaffirm requests microphone access only to record affirmations, and only when you tap record. Audio stays on your device unless you have an account, in which case it is backed up as described above.

Children

Lunaffirm is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.
