Lunaffirm

Help & FAQ

Everything you need to get restful nights with Lunaffirm.

How do I record an affirmation?

Go to the Record tab, pick a theme, choose a starter script or write your own, then tap the microphone and read it aloud in a slow, calm voice. Tap stop, listen back, and tap Save affirmation. It lands in your Library.

How does "tonight's mix" work?

From Home or Library, tap Play tonight's mix. On the Sleep screen, the Looping tonight dropdown lets you pick one affirmation to loop all night, with a gentle gap of silence between repeats over your chosen background sound.

Can I play just sounds with no affirmation?

Yes — on the Sleep screen tap Just play sounds — no affirmations to play only the ambient bed.

What are the background sounds?

Ocean Waves, Rain, White Noise, Brown Noise, and Crickets (or Off). The background gently lowers in volume while your voice plays and rises again in the gaps.

How does the sleep timer work?

Choose 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes. When it ends, the audio fades out over about eight seconds and stops.

What is the gentle wake alarm?

In You → Sleep, turn on the gentle wake alarm, set a time, pick a sound (or your own voice), and a fade-in length. Lunaffirm keeps a quiet audio session running overnight so the sound can fade in with the screen locked. Keep your phone charging and don't force-quit the app overnight for best results.

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to You → Subscription → Restore purchases, or reinstall and sign in with the same Apple ID.

How do I change my name?

You → Account & privacy → Your name.

How do I delete my data?

You → Account & privacy → Delete all data. This permanently removes your recordings and stats from the device.
