Delete your account & data

Lunaffirm by Chehab Group LLC · Last updated: October 16, 2026

You can delete your Lunaffirm account and the data linked to it at any time, on iPhone or Android.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lunaffirm and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap Account & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete all data and confirm.

This permanently deletes your recordings, stats, and settings from your device and from our cloud.

Option 2: By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email info@chehab-group.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Lunaffirm account". If you signed in with Apple or Google, tell us which one you used. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your voice recordings and affirmation titles
  • Your settings, stats, and in-app activity linked to your account
  • Your email address held by our subscription provider (RevenueCat)

What we may keep

Records of purchases and subscription transactions may be kept as long as required for tax, accounting, and legal purposes. These are not linked to your recordings.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings (iPhone) or the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions (Android).
